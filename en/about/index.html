Research Work

GLM-4.5 / GLM-5

Core Contributor

I am responsible for developing GLM's deep-search capabilities. I create question-answer data from knowledge graphs and web pages, and filter model search logs for training. I use this data for supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, improve how models retain and use information across searches, and evaluate the results on BrowseComp and other benchmarks.

BrowseComp is OpenAI’s benchmark for browsing agents.

About Me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yuxiao Dong. I studied at Tsinghua from 2021 to 2025, earning dual bachelor's degrees in Mathematics and Physics (B.Sc.) and Software Engineering (B.Eng.), before entering the Ph.D. program directly.

Research Interests

01

Deep Research

I study how models answer questions through multiple searches. This includes preparing question-answer data, training search strategies with reinforcement learning, and retaining useful information from earlier searches.

02

LLM Post-Training

I use supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to train models to use tools and write code, and evaluate how well they complete tasks.

03

Multi-Agent Systems

I study how agents divide up search and coding tasks, and how to organize their execution and training so they spend less time waiting for one another.

AsyncSwarm

AsyncSwarm: Asynchronous Multi-Agent Training and Inference for Information Seeking

First Author 2026

Rui Lu*, Zhenyu Hou*, Dan Zhang, Pengfan Du, Yujiang Li, Jie Tang, Yuxiao Dong

AsyncSwarm studies asynchronous multi-agent training and collaboration. Agents can search independently and reuse earlier search records for training, reducing the time spent waiting for one another.

AgentRL: Scaling Agentic Reinforcement Learning with a Multi-Turn, Multi-Task Framework

Hanchen Zhang, Xiao Liu, Bowen Lv, Xueqiao Sun, Bohao Jing, Iat Long Iong, Zhenyu Hou, Zehan Qi, Hanyu Lai, Yifan Xu, Rui Lu, Hongning Wang, Jie Tang, Yuxiao Dong

DeepDive: Advancing Deep Search Agents with Knowledge Graphs and Multi-Turn RL

arXiv 2509.10446 Code 352Stars Dataset 23,751total downloads

DeepDive builds challenging questions from knowledge graphs and trains models to search for answers through multi-turn reinforcement learning, with a penalty for unnecessary searches. Read the Chinese note ↗

The question-generation pipeline, datasets, and training code are open source. This work is used in post-training the GLM family.
